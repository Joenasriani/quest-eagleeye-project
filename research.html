Project EagleEye: Low-Cost Mixed-Reality Situational Awareness Research

A technical research project by Joe Nasr / QuestRequestVR exploring how Meta Quest 3, thermal sensing, LoRa/Meshtastic concepts, gaze interaction and consumer hardware can be combined into an experimental wearable spatial interface.

Interactive project · GitHub repository · Quest Research collection · Author record

Status: technical research / experimental systems study. It explores selected low-cost situational-awareness interface functions; it does not claim functional equivalence with specialized military systems.

Research question

Which mixed-reality situational-awareness functions can be prototyped with consumer off-the-shelf hardware, and what integration trade-offs appear when sensing, communications, power and immersive display are combined in a wearable system?

Technical frame

The concept joins Meta Quest 3 mixed reality, external thermal sensing, LoRa/Meshtastic-style communications, USB-C power/data integration and gaze-driven interaction. This makes it a systems-integration problem spanning hardware, XR, networking, sensing and HCI.

Who this is useful for

Meta Quest engineers, XR and VR developers, AI builders, tech builders, robotics and sensing experimenters, spatial-computing teams, hardware-software integrators, simulation developers, creative technologists, technical educators and advanced VR enthusiasts.

Why AI and robotics builders may care

AI and robotics systems often combine perception, communication and operator decision-making. EagleEye is relevant as an interface case study: how sensor-derived information and peer/device data might be surfaced to a human in context through a wearable mixed-reality display.

Evidence boundary

Any cost, latency, range, battery or performance figure should be treated as project-reported unless the test protocol, hardware/firmware versions, environmental conditions, repeated measurements and underlying results are published. The work is technical research, not a certification or safety claim.
